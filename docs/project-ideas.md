# CAB302 Project Idea Development

## Reuben Brainstorm
Eco-habits Learning Companion/Game (#17/#18 variation):
- Purpose: incentivise users to develop sustainable behaviours by gamifying behaviours and activities which provide positive environmental and social outcomes. 
  - Relates to people (positive mental and social benefit), planet (promotes eco-friendly behaviour)
- Features: 
  - Level-based system which ties completion of activities to gained xp and coins (or a similar system)
  - coins can be used to purchase in-game valuables/tokens 
  - xp contributes to the players progress in terms of levels and titles 
  - Based on user-defined focugit shortlog --summary --numbered --all --no-mergess/goals (social, environmental, economic), AI creates activities/goals 
  - Activity: a one-off exercise/project user can engage with – read a resource, trim their garden, etc. 
  - Goal: Hitting a target over time – drink 2L of water today, put $500 into savings this month 
  - Graphed progress over time – how many activities were completed this week vs. last week 
  - Project how many activities will be completed this month, this year, etc. 
  - Maintain journal of activities engaged with
