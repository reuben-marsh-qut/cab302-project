# How You Worked as a Team

Our two meetings each week, one in person on Monday and the other virtual on Friday were an essential part of our teamwork.
On Monday we held a retrospective on the previous week's tasks and set new tasks. Friday was a primarily a check in where we made sure that we were all on track to complete our tasks for the coming Monday meeting.

Outside of those times, we communicated over Instagram.
