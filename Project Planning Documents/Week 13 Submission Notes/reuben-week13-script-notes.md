## Week 13 Submission Script Notes

### Keep in mind:

- 5 minutes presentation, 6 presenters 
  - 50 seconds per person ~ 150 words -> 900 words (total)
- Don't discuss software/libraries you were told to use (i.e. don't need to talk about JavaFX)


### Dicussion points

- Who is the intended user?
- What is the need, opportunity, gap or motivation?
- How did you work as a team?
  * how were roles split and shared? -> think scrum master, product owner rotation
  * habits and practices -> evolution over the assessment
* How was dev. work organised/managed across the assessment?
* How were technical and design decisions made?
* What did 'quality' mean throughout the project?
  * How did we foster a high 'quality' work environment? And how did this improve the app quality
* What were the most important things you learnt? challenges?
* What didn't get done?
