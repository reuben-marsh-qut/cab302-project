## Experience Logic
This document briefly outlines how XP will be calculated based on completion of activities, habits and goals.

Activities:
Activities have XP associated with them based on their difficulty (level 1-10). TODO: what constitutes a level 1 - 10 activity

| Level | XP  |
|-------|-----|
| 1     | 10  |
| 2     | 20  |
| 3     | 30  |
| 4     | 40  |
| 5     | 50  |
| 6     | 60  |
| 7     | 70  |
| 8     | 80  |
| 9     | 90  |
| 10    | 100 |

Habits:
XP is set based on streak milestone, not the difficulty of the composite activities - this is because the difficulty of the activities defines how much xp you'll get for them, so we don't really nee dto double-count that 

At 50 day intervals, get 100 XP, at 100 day intervals get 200 XP.

| Days | XP  |
|------|-----|
| 7    | 50  |
| 25   | 75  |
| 50   | 100 |
| 100  | 200 |
| 150  | 100 |
| 200  | 200 |
